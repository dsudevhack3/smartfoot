System Architecture & Known Limitations

SmartFoot IoT Diabetic Foot Monitoring Platform Specifications

Mandatory Medical Disclaimer

"SmartFoot is a monitoring tool for educational and research purposes. It is not a medical device and has not been validated for clinical use. Do not use for medical diagnosis or treatment decisions. Consult qualified healthcare professionals for medical advice."

Known Platform Limitations

  • Sensor Calibration Requirement: FSR pressure sensors and DS18B20 thermal probes require periodic zero-point calibration for optimal clinical precision.
  • Configurable Risk Thresholds: Risk score thresholds (LOW <30, MODERATE 30-60, HIGH >60) are configurable research parameters and explicitly not clinically validated FDA thresholds.
  • Concurrency Scope: Single-file SQLite database configuration is designed for research demonstrations; production scale deployment requires PostgreSQL.
  • Regulatory Status: Not FDA approved. Strictly intended for research, prototype evaluation, and observational demonstration.
  • Demo Mode Authentication: Static OTP code (`123456`) is provided for testing convenience in demo mode and must be swapped with SMTP email delivery prior to production deployment.
